## Highlights
---
September is here! openwashdata team is preparing new datasets, attending WASH events, and planning community events. You could [find where we are](https://openwashdata.org/pages/events/) if not in front of computers hacking R. When in office, we have started some long-term datasets development. For an overview of data packages that are published or in development, [see our Data Warehouse](https://openwashdata.org/pages/gallery/data/).

### 🎙️first qualitative data package released!
Do you know people that work with qualitative data (e.g. semi-structured interviews)? Have you ever talked to them about publishing their data (transcripts)?

Ask them what they think of this: https://openwashdata.github.io/biogasoutcomesmalawi/ and leave the thoughts to ghe@mavt.ethz.ch!

We have gone through the process of anonymisation, converting .docx files to .md (markdown) files, and then publishing the transcripts, as well as a table format of the data that is accessible as CSV, XLSX and our favourite R package format.

### 🌱community is growing  
We are happy to see the expanding of the openwashdata community after launch:
- 9 people joined our community chat
- 11 people signed up for the newsletter
- 2 new datasets are published
  
Besdies, we are working on a few others to be published soon. Topics include data for:
- an analysis of the suitability of container-based sanitation in Haiti
- an assessment of global demand for different methods of faecal sludge emptying
- weekly volumes of water pumped for hand pumps in Kenya

### 🇸🇪openwashdata team at World Water Week
Sebastian and Mian attended World Water Week in Stockholm to exchange ideas with and be inspired with WASH professionals! In this year's keynote, data, especially data integration, is a key component for innovation and financing. Furthermore, local data is needed to lift up and transfer indigeneous knowledge as well as to provide forsights to create new knowledge. openwashdata is dedicated to empower this and share WASH data towards achieving SDG goal 6.
