### 🔬 New dataset `rwpfunctionality` released!
The dataset `rwpfunctionality` is about water point monitoring data associated with the paper "Rural water point functionality estimates and associations: evidence from nine countries in sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia"(Murray, Anna L et al., 2024).
- [`rwpfunctionality`](https://openwashdata.github.io/rwpfunctionality): Water point functionality assessment in nine sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia countries.

## openwashdata Blog: Partnership with SOIL
---
We are proud to announce the strategic partnership with SOIL, a non-profit organization working in Haiti to combat sanitation crisis. openwashdata and SOIL have formed a strategic partnership to collaborate on data stewardship. Together, we will assess existing data management practices, formulate a preliminary data management strategy, and pinpoint datasets ready for open publication.

[Read more here](https://openwashdata.org/pages/blog/posts/2024-04-02-partnership-soil/)
