## Highlights
---
### 🏆 80+ countries, 120+ attendees, and 200+ registrations: A successful kick-off of Data Science for openwashdata!
On 31st of October, our first edition of Data Science for openwashdata started!
With more than 200 registrations from all over the world, our first module, spanning two and a half hours, witnessed an astounding 120+ participants brimming with enthusiasm and curiosity! 
Our first cohort is diverse and covers more than 80 countries with many from the global south. 
Last week, the participants delved into the basics of open data tools such as the Quarto documenting system, version control and GitHub, R programming and more journeys await ahead!
The successful kick-off has left us more thrilled, excited, and motivated to steer this academy on a path of continuous growth and learning!
