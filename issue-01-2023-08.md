### 🧭 Roadmap of openwashdata and how we get there
Everyday the Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) sector works towards better global health.
To achieve this, WASH practitioners bring expertise into developing better policy, engineering, and research where data is one of the unsung hero to support the successes. 
However, a huge amount of washdata is often left behind silent due to a lack of up-to-date data expertise.

Openwashdata is here to unmute.
The project will strategically enhance the data competency and promote open science in WASH domain by 
- Mobilizing a data community: creating diverse channels to engage people to share and learn from each other (e.g., Matrix chatroom, learner blog series)
- Facilatating/Streamlining? data sharing and cleaning: developing user-directed reproducible dataset tables and R data packages
- Communicating data practice: publishing citable data products and hosting educational events for the WASH community.

[Read more on our funding proposal here](https://openwashdata.org/pages/proposal/)

### 🥳 First openwashdata R data package released!
We have released our first R data package `fsmglobal` with example code snippets! Check [`fsmglobal`](https://openwashdata.github.io/fsmglobal/) package out! This dataset is part of the work [Global Faecal Sludge Emptying Services Demand (Greene et al. 2021)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jenvman.2021.112612), the first study to quantify the service needs for 772 M onsite sanitation facilities. Thanks for the efforts of all data collectors and authors!
