### 🧭 Roadmap of openwashdata and How We Get There

Every day the Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH) sector works towards better global health.

To achieve this, WASH practitioners bring expertise into developing better policy, engineering, and research where data is one of the unsung hero to support the successes. However, a great amount of data is only ever used once and remains unpublished due to a lack of up-to-date data expertise. openwashdata is here to chnage that. 

This community will strategically enhance the data competency and promote open principles in the WASH domain by:

- Mobilizing a data community: creating diverse channels to engage people to share and learn from each other (e.g., Matrix chatroom, learner blog series).
- Facilitating data sharing and cleaning: developing user-directed reproducible datasets as R data packages.
- Communicating data practice: publishing citable data products and hosting educational events for the WASH community.

[Read more on our funded proposal here](https://openwashdata.org/pages/proposal/)

### 🥳 First Data Package Released!

We have released our first R data package `wasteskipsblantyre` with example code snippets! Check [`wasteskipsblantyre` package](https://openwashdata.github.io/wasteskipsblantyre/) out! This dataset includes locations of the publicly accessible waste skips in Blantyre, Malawi. The data was collected in 2021. Thanks for the efforts of all data collectors and authors!
