Since the launch of openwashdata, we published the following datasets shared from diverse sources: academic research, government open data, NGO, and private data donators. We have published different types of data too, such as engineering data, qualitative data (), 

- [rwpfunctionality](https://openwashdata.github.io/rwpfunctionality):	Water point monitoring data associated with the paper "Rural water point functionality estimates and associations: evidence from nine countries in sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia".
- [gdho](https://openwashdata.github.io/rwpfunctionality): A dataset of global humanitarian organizations collected by Humanitarian Outcomes.
- [chckapmalawi](https://openwashdata.github.io/chckapmalawi):	Community Health Club Knowledge Attitude Practice Survey Malawi
- [cleanteamghana](https://openwashdata.github.io/cleanteamghana):	User experience of the Clean Team Ghana’s container-based sanitation service in Kumasi, Ghana.
- [basisghana](https://openwashdata.github.io/basisghana): Open defecation free (ODF) communities in Ghana in 2017.
- [universityrankingafrica](https://openwashdata.github.io/universityrankingafrica): Data from institutional and national levels was combined into a dataset to provide a comprehensive characterization of the top ranked Universities of every African country.
- [waterpumpkwale](https://openwashdata.github.io/waterpumpkwale):	Weekly volume of water pumped for handpumps monitored with Smart Handpump technology, Kwale County, Kenya
- [grdwtrsmpkwale](https://openwashdata.github.io/grdwtrsmpkwale):	Groundwater analysis from 2016 in Kwale, Kenya
- [cbssuitabilityhaiti](https://openwashdata.github.io/cbssuitabilityhaiti): Spatial data to support an analysis of suitability of container-based sanitation in flood prone areas of Haiti.
- [biogasoutcomesmalawi](https://openwashdata.github.io/biogasoutcomesmalawi):	Data for 61 semi-structured interviews with biogas owners in Malawi.
- [wasteskipsblantyre](https://openwashdata.github.io/wasteskipsblantyre):	Data on locations of the publicly accessible waste skips in Blantyre, Malawi collected in 2021.
- [fsmglobal](https://openwashdata.github.io/fsmglobal): Populations of 175 countries requiring different faecal sludge emptying services.

## openwashdata Blog: Creating and visualizing maps of WASH data (II)
What if our data only contains geographical information like countries or regions? This second blog tutorial will guide you to combine your data with useful external geographical data to plot the maps. We walk you through with an example on drawing the global distribution of humanitarian organizations with openwashdata package [`gdho`](https://github.com/openwashdata/gdho/).
