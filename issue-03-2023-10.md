### 🌊 Open data science academy for WASH! Register Now
Are you a WASH professional, student or enthusiast seeking to empower yourself with cutting-edge **data skills**?

Dive into the world of open data and make a splash in the WASH domain! Join our very first Open Data Science Academy on a mission to equip WASH enthusiasts with the tools and workflows for open data and code. In this free course, you'll master the art of reproducible data analysis while embracing open science principles. With flexible commitments and 1:1 coding support, you'll be on the path to transforming the data ecosystem of WASH sector. Don't miss out on this unique opportunity to know the like-minded fellows and shape the future of WASH data together!

- Date: 2023-10-31 to 2024-01-16 
- Time: Every Tuesday 2pm (CET) 
- Format: Online via Zoom Meeting
- Cost: FREE!
