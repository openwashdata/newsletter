# Get Involved Section Content

## Complete "Get Involved" Section

We believe the openwashdata project prospers when we have **YOU** work
together and promote open science and data practice! No matter what
background you are from, we come up with some ways for you to get
involved:

### Connect & Collaborate
- [Join our chatroom to meet
  people!](https://openwashdata.org/pages/get-started/chat/)
- Got ideas or questions? [Leave us a message on Matrix to
  collaborate!](https://matrix.to/#/%23openwashdata-lobby:staffchat.ethz.ch)

### Share & Contribute Data
- **[Share your WASH data with
  us](https://openwashdata.org/pages/blog/posts/2024-05-17-data-publication-1/)**
  - Follow our step-by-step guide to prepare and submit your dataset
  - We'll help ensure your data meets FAIR principles
  - Get a DOI and make your data citable
  
- **Write a data dictionary/codebook**
  - Document your variables clearly: name, description, units, and
    allowed values
  - Use our [data dictionary
    template](https://github.com/openwashdata/data-dictionary-template)
  - Include metadata about data collection methods and limitations

### Cite & Use Our Work
- **How to cite datasets**: Each dataset has a DOI and citation info on
  its homepage (e.g., `https://openwashdata.github.io/[package-name]`)
- **How to cite the project**: Use our [Zenodo
  record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8300700) for the openwashdata
  project
- **Use our data in courses/training**: All datasets are CC-BY licensed
  and perfect for teaching. Contact us for support materials or
  workshop ideas

### Report & Suggest
- **Submit an idea or issue**:
  - For specific datasets: Use the GitHub Issues tab on the dataset
    repository
  - For general suggestions: Open an issue on our [main
    repository](https://github.com/openwashdata/openwashdata.github.io/issues)
  - No GitHub account? Email us at openwashdata@ethz.ch

### Spread the Word
- Forward this newsletter to colleagues
- Share our datasets on social media
- Present openwashdata at conferences or workshops

## Individual Guide Templates

### How to Share a Dataset with Us
1. Prepare your data following FAIR principles
2. Create a data dictionary documenting all variables
3. Contact us via email (openwashdata@ethz.ch) or Matrix
4. We'll guide you through the submission process
5. Your dataset will receive a DOI and be fully citable

### How to Cite Datasets or the Project
**For individual datasets:**
- Visit the dataset homepage:
  `https://openwashdata.github.io/[package-name]`
- Look for the citation section
- Copy the provided citation in your preferred format

**For the openwashdata project:**
```
Zhong, M., Kalina, M., & Lüthi, C. (2023). openwashdata: An 
ecosystem for open WASH data. Zenodo. 
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8300700
```

### How to Submit an Idea or Issue
1. **With GitHub account**: Navigate to the relevant repository and
   click "Issues" → "New Issue"
2. **Without GitHub account**: Email us at openwashdata@ethz.ch with
   your suggestion
3. Include: Clear description, context, and any relevant examples

### Using Our Data in Courses or Training
- All datasets are CC-BY licensed - free to use with attribution
- Perfect for: Data analysis courses, WASH research training, R/Python
  tutorials
- Contact us for: Teaching materials, workshop support, guest lectures
- Explore our [data science course](https://ds4owd-002.github.io/website/)

### How to Write a Data Dictionary
1. List all variables in your dataset
2. For each variable, document:
   - Variable name (machine-readable)
   - Description (human-readable)
   - Units (if applicable)
   - Data type (numeric, text, date, etc.)
   - Allowed values or ranges
   - Missing value codes
3. Include metadata about collection methods and known limitations
4. Use our [template](https://github.com/openwashdata/data-dictionary-template)
