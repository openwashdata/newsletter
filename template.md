## Get Involved

We believe the openwashdata project prospers when we have **YOU** work
together and promote open science and data practice! Here's how you can
get involved:

- **Connect**: [Join our
  chatroom](https://openwashdata.org/pages/get-started/chat/) | [Matrix
  collaboration](https://matrix.to/#/%23openwashdata-lobby:staffchat.ethz.ch)
- **Share data**: [Submit your WASH
  dataset](https://openwashdata.org/pages/blog/posts/2024-05-17-data-publication-1/)
  | [Data dictionary
  template](https://github.com/openwashdata/data-dictionary-template)
- **Use & cite**: Find citation info on each dataset page | [Cite the
  project](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8300700)
- **Contribute**: [Report issues or suggest
  ideas](https://github.com/openwashdata/openwashdata.github.io/issues)
  | Email: openwashdata@ethz.ch
- **Spread the word**: Forward this newsletter | Share on social media
