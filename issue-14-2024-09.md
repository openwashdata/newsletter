Welcome to the September edition of our newsletter, packed with two released datasets, and a blog post about creating maps!

## 🌊 Three new datasets released! Uganda, Malawi, Brazil, Cote d'Ivoire, Peru

- [`watercostaccra`](https://openwashdata.github.io/watercostaccra/): Data of household survey on water costs and coping strategies in Accra, Ghana.
- [`whatawaste`](https://openwashdata.github.io/waschoolpiracema/): Data about a global project What a Waste that aggregated data on solid waste management from around the world.

## openwashdata Blog: Creating and visualizing maps of WASH data (III)
---
Curious about creating interactive maps in R for pumps, water points and more? This final blog post is a must-read! The tutorial will guide you step-by-step how to plot and visualize an interactive map with your geospatioal data using `leaflet` library. We walk you through with an example on drawing the distribution of the water pumps in Kwale, Kenya with the openwashdata package `waterpumpkwale`.

[Continue to read here](https://openwashdata.org/pages/blog/posts/2024-03-01-create-map-part-3/)
