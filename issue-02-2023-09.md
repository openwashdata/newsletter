## Highlights
---
September is here! openwashdata team is preparing new datasets, attending WASH events, and planning community events. You could [find where we are](https://openwashdata.org/pages/events/) if not in front of computers hacking R. When in office, we have started some long-term datasets development. For an overview of data packages that are published or in development, [see our Data Warehouse](https://openwashdata.org/pages/gallery/data/).
### 🌱community is growing  
We are happy to see the expanding of the openwashdata community after launch:
- 9 people joined our community chat
- 11 people signed up for the newsletter
- 2 new datasets are published
  
Besdies, we are working on a few others to be published soon. Topics include data for:
- an analysis of the suitability of container-based sanitation in Haiti
- an assessment of global demand for different methods of faecal sludge emptying
- weekly volumes of water pumped for hand pumps in Kenya

We truly believe that openwashdata project prospers when we have **YOU** work together and promote open science and data practice! No matter what background you are from, we come up with some ways for you to get involved:

- [Join our chatroom to meet people!](https://openwashdata.org/pages/get-started/chat/)

- Give stars to openwashdata on Github.
- Spread the word. Forward this email.
