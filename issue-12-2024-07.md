## 🚀 Hackathon
On June 21st, openwashdata successfully hosted the first data package hackathon. All participants delivered a data package in just 8 hours. The datasets span from Malawi 🇲🇼 to Uganda 🇺🇬, Brazil 🇧🇷 to Peru 🇵🇪, and Ghana 🇬🇭 to beyond. The participants were introduced to the openwashdata workflow of data package development by [Mian Zhong](https://openwashdata.org/about/mian/). Interested in what they achieved? 

Check out the showcase at: https://openwashdata.org/pages/events/2024-06-21-hackathon/
