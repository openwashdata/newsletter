## 🚀 First Data Package Hackathon Success
On June 21st, openwashdata successfully hosted the [first data package hackathon](https://openwashdata.org/pages/events/2024-06-21-hackathon). Six data enthusiats gathered in Zurich to work on diverse WASH datasets from countries like Malawi 🇲🇼, Uganda 🇺🇬, Brazil 🇧🇷, Peru 🇵🇪, and Ghana 🇬🇭. In a hands-on session, the participants were introduced to the openwashdata workflow of data package development by [Mian Zhong](https://openwashdata.org/about/mian/). Within just 8 hours, every participant delivered an impressive data package. 

Curious about what they achieved or the secrets behind the workflow? [Discover the showcase and insights](https://openwashdata.org/pages/events/2024-06-21-hackathon/#showcase)

## 🎓 Congrats to 20 graduates data science for openwashdata
Congratulations! By completing a capstone project, we have 20 graduates for [data science for openwashdata](https://github.com/ds4owd-001). The capstone project shows their learning of R, tidyverse and Quarto and their ability to apply these skills to real-world problems. The projects cover diverse WASH topics with data from various regions and sources. 

[Check out our graduates capstone projects](https://openwashdata.org/pages/academy/graduates/)
