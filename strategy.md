# Newsletter Strategy (Phase 1)

The first phase of the newsletter focuses on informing the community of our infrastruture and long-term goals.

**Objectives:** - Highlight the initial project milestones - Demonstrate open science practice - Get more participants

## Content

To achieve the goals, we develop the newsletter content with three main components: (1) Project Highlight, (2) Practice Tip (Blog post), and (3) Get Involved. We want to include some of the optional sections once more people are engaged and events are finalized.
