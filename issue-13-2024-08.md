## [emoji] 5 new datasets released! Uganda, Malawi, Brazil, Cote d'Ivoire, Peru

- [`ugabore`](https://github.com/openwashdata/ugabore): Borehole repair data from central Uganda 
- [`boreholefuncmwi`](https://github.com/openwashdata/boreholefuncmwi): Data about the survey on the borehole functionality in Karonga district, Malawi.
- [`waschoolpiracema`](https://openwashdata.github.io/waschoolpiracema/): Data about WASH in Schools in Piracema, Brazil
- [`saniabidjan`](https://openwashdata.github.io/saniabidjan): Data about household survey of managing sanitation facilities in Abidjan, Cote d’Ivoire
- [`portawaterperu`](https://openwashdata.github.io/saniabidjan): Data about Peruvian Portable Water System
