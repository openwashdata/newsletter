## Highlights

### 📊 Three new datasets released

We have 3 new datasets released that cover data about open research in WASH🔬, humanitarian organizations⛑️, and solid wasted🚮!
Our previous released datasets are mostly focusing on experiment data. 
These three datasets are valuable high-level data that bridge the gap between research/technical work and practice.

- [`washopenresearch`](https://openwashdata.github.io/washopenresearch): overview of open research data related to Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH)
- [`gdho`](https://openwashdata.github.io/gdho): global database of humanitarian organizations from Humanitarian Outcomes
- [`whatawaste`](https://openwashdata.github.io/whatawaste): data about a global project What a Waste that aggregated data on solid waste management from around the world

Do you know what's more unique?
The dataset `washopenresearch` is an initiative from openwashdata team that we collect, analyze, and will update regularly about the current landscape of open research data in the greater WASH domain. 
To our best knowledge, this is the first dataset to reflect the data availability of WASH research and we welcome your suggestions and feedbacks. Stay tuned!
