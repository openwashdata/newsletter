## Highlights

### 📊 Three new datasets released

We have 3 new datasets released that cover data about humanitarian organizations⛑️, open research in WASH🔬, and solid wasted🚮!

- [`washopenresearch`](https://openwashdata.github.io/washopenresearch): overview of open research data related to Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH)
- [`gdho`](https://openwashdata.github.io/gdho): global database of humanitarian organizations from Humanitarian Outcomes
- [`whatawaste`](https://openwashdata.github.io/whatawaste): data about a global project What a Waste that aggregated data on solid waste management from around the world
