🌻 Welcome to the June issue. We want to share some new datasets including [the first Dataset about Open Research Data in WASH](https://openwashdata.github.io/washopenresearch).


## 💧Two new datasets Released! 
We have two new datasets released for you about open research and investment in WASH! Presented by openwashdata team, `washopenresearch` is the first dataset investigating the data availability of publications in the WASH sector. This first version reviews two sources and it will be expanded in the future versions. Check them out 🤩:

- [washopenresearch](https://openwashdata.github.io/washopenresearch): Dataset about open research data availability in Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH)
- [washinvestments](https://openwashdata.github.io/washinvestments)：Dataset on multilateral development bank (MDB) investment in water supply and sanitation.
